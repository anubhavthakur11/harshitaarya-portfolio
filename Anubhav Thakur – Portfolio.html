My roadmap to 2030

These are my goals for each year of the degree, not things already done. I will update them as I complete each one.

  1. Year 1 · 2026–27

    Build the foundation

    • Settle into LPU and aim for a strong CGPA in the core subjects
    • Learn Drug Formulation and Delivery Systems basics by mid 2027
    • Get fluent in MS Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Canva
    • Start GPAT and GATE preparation early, with a light weekly routine
  2. Year 2 · 2027–28

    Go hands-on in the hospital

    • Hospital Pharmacy internship at Fortis / Max Healthcare
    • Learn pharmacology, medication reconciliation and patient counselling
    • Start pharmaceutical analysis: HPLC and UV spectroscopy
    • Understand Medicinal Chemistry and Drug Design basics by 2028
  3. Year 3 · 2028–29

    Learn drug safety and data

    • Pharmacovigilance Programme of India internship at the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission, Ghaziabad
    • Practise ADR reporting and toxicology
    • Add Basic Python for Data Analysis and biostatistics tools
    • Work on a research poster or paper
  4. Year 4 · 2029–30

    Step into industry

    • Production and Quality Assurance internship at Cipla or Glenmark, covering GMP
    • Final-year project in formulation
    • Use ChemDraw and literature search tools for research work
    • Sit GPAT / GATE and apply for placements, with AstraZeneca as the top target
  5. After 2030

    Specialise and lead

    • M.Pharm in Pharmaceutics, or MS in Pharmaceutical Technology
    • Formulation and Process Development Scientist role
    • Grow toward leading a pharma R&D team
